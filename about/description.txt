Browse your past runs on the game's own end-of-run result screen, from the title menu.

The game keeps your last 20 results and overwrites the rest. This mod archives every run
to disk as it finishes, so your history keeps going past that cap, and it reuses the real
result screen to show them. Same layout, same stat bars, same tooltips on skills, memories
and gems. Nothing is redrawn or approximated.

What you get:
- "Run History" entry in the title menu
- a scrollable list of every archived run, with date, result, length, players and difficulty
- click a run to open it, arrows to switch between players in that run
- hover anything for the same tooltip you get at the end of a match
- very short runs and quick abandons are filtered out, the header says how many are hidden

Does not change gameplay. No new items, no balance changes, nothing sent over the network.
It only reads results the game already saved, and the window can only be opened outside a
match. Runs are archived under:

  %USERPROFILE%\AppData\LocalLow\Lizard Smoothie\Shape of Dreams\RunHistory\

One caveat: archiving starts when you install the mod. Runs you played before that are
imported from the game's own last-20 list, so you keep what the game still remembers, but
anything already overwritten is gone and there's no way to get it back.

Source: https://github.com/yurivault/DewRunHistory
