Look at your old runs on the game's own result screen, straight from the title menu.

Shape of Dreams keeps your last 20 results and quietly overwrites everything older, and
once you click past the end-of-run screen there's no way back to it. This writes every run
to disk the moment it ends, then clones the real result screen to show them back. Same
layout, same stat bars, same tooltips on skills, memories and gems. Nothing is redrawn or
approximated.

What you get:
- a "Run History" entry in the title menu
- a scrollable list of every archived run: date, result, length, players, difficulty
- click a run to open it, arrows switch between players in that run
- hover anything for the same tooltip you get at the end of a match
- very short runs and quick abandons are filtered out, the header says how many are hidden

Safe to run:
- no gameplay changes, it only reads results the game already saved
- no MOD badge on your lobby, that comes from isGameplayAltered and this doesn't set it
- nothing goes over the network
- it won't open during a match, since the game already shows you all of this while you play

Worth knowing: Steam cross-play switches off while any mod is active. That's the game's
rule, not this mod's, and it applies to every mod on the Workshop.

Read this before installing: archiving starts when you install. On first launch it imports
whatever the game still remembers, so you keep those last 20. Anything already overwritten
is gone for good. Install it before your next good run, not after.

Runs live in, one JSON each, and they survive uninstalling the mod:
  %USERPROFILE%\AppData\LocalLow\Lizard Smoothie\Shape of Dreams\RunHistory\
